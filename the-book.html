The Foundation

About the Book

Why do successful organizations suddenly find themselves off course? The answer rarely lies in a catastrophic failure, but rather in a slow, almost imperceptible shift known as Leadership Drift.

The book examines the subtle behavioral compromises, communication breakdowns, and overloaded priorities that pull leaders away from operational discipline.

Drawing on decades of experience, Leadership Drift provides not only a diagnosis of this pervasive issue but a concrete, actionable framework to realign your teams, re-establish trust, and build a resilient culture that can weather any storm.
