Founder Story & Advisory Mission

Building Leadership Systems That Scale

Strong leadership isn't about charisma or constant firefighting. It's about designing disciplined operating systems that allow teams and individuals to execute with clarity, confidence, and speed.

As Founder and Principal of KLP Consulting Services, L.L.C. and author of Leadership Drift, Kristen Peloquin brings over two decades of hands-on experience leading operations, advising executive teams, and scaling high-performing organizations.

Throughout her career across complex corporate environments, Kristen observed that most operational friction isn’t caused by a lack of effort or talent. It happens when systems fail to keep pace with growth—when decision rights blur, priority pipelines overflow, and cross-departmental handoffs break down.

She founded KLP Consulting Services to equip senior executives, emerging leaders, and ambitious professionals with the practical tools, direct feedback, and structured advisory needed to eliminate operational drag, accelerate careers, and build sustainable momentum.

Kristen's advisory focuses on real-world mechanics: clear decision ownership (the Rule of the Single “A”), capacity discipline, and hard operating boundaries that hold up under pressure. Whether through direct executive coaching, corporate leadership offsites, comprehensive practice audits, or enterprise implementation of Leadership Drift, Kristen helps leaders achieve more by leading better.
