Founder Story & Advisory Mission

Building Leadership Systems That Scale

Strong leadership isn't about charisma or constant firefighting. It's about designing disciplined operating systems that allow teams to execute with clarity, confidence, and speed.

As the founder and CEO of KLP Consulting Services and author of Leadership Drift, Kristen Peloquin brings over two decades of hands-on experience leading operations, advising executive teams, and scaling high-performing organizations.

Throughout my career across complex corporate environments, I’ve observed that most operational friction isn’t caused by a lack of effort or talent. It happens when systems fail to keep pace with growth—when decision rights blur, priority pipelines overflow, and handoffs between departments break down.

I founded KLP Consulting Services to give senior executives and emerging leaders the practical tools, direct feedback, and structured advisory needed to eliminate operational drag and build sustainable momentum.

My approach focuses on real-world mechanics: clear decision ownership, capacity discipline, and hard operating boundaries that hold up under pressure. Whether through 1-on-1 executive coaching, comprehensive practice audits, or interactive team workshops, my mission is to help organizations achieve more by leading better.
