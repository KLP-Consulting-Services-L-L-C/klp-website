Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • KLP Consulting Services, L.L.C.

1. Overview & Commitment to Confidentiality

KLP Consulting Services, L.L.C. (“we,” “our,” or “us”) operates klpconsultingsvcs.org and related reader resources for the published title Leadership Drift. We recognize that executive advisory and leadership diagnostics require the highest standard of confidentiality and discretion. We treat your personal and organizational information with the utmost respect.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us through our website. This includes:

  • Contact & Advisory Inquiries: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and notes submitted via our contact forms or scheduling portal.
  • Reader Portal Registration: Name and email address submitted to unlock companion operating frameworks and downloadable tools for Leadership Drift.
  • Usage & Analytics Data: Non-personally identifiable technical information such as browser type, referring pages, approximate geographic location, and interactions with buttons (such as Amazon or Goodreads links) to help us measure and improve website performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate business and client communication purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries regarding executive advisory, workshops, speaking engagements, or enterprise licensing.
  • To deliver companion operating tools, templates, and updates related to Leadership Drift.
  • To maintain client records and deliver agreed-upon professional services.
  • We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your contact or business information to any third party under any circumstances.

4. Third-Party Platforms & Links

Our website includes external links to third-party platforms such as Amazon (for book purchases and Amazon Attribution analytics), Goodreads, LinkedIn, and scheduling software. When you click these links, you navigate to an external site governed by that third party's privacy policy and terms. We encourage you to review their respective policies.

5. Data Security & Retention

We take appropriate administrative and technical precautions to safeguard the information submitted through our site. We retain inquiry information only as long as necessary to conduct business, fulfill requested advisory services, or comply with applicable legal obligations.
