How to Paste into Outlook:
  1. Click "Copy to Clipboard" on either signature above.
  2. In Outlook, go to File > Options > Mail > Signatures (or on Mac / Web: Settings > Mail > Signatures).
  3. Delete the old text completely in the signature box.
  4. Press Ctrl + V (or right-click and choose "Keep Source Formatting").
  5. Click Save.
