Leadership Drift

Even the most seasoned leaders can lose their course. Leadership Drift explores the subtle shifts that pull organizations off track and provides a clear, actionable framework for course correction. Discover the principles that will realign your leadership and propel your team forward.

Drawing on decades of executive experience, Kristen Peloquin provides concrete operating tools—including the Decision Rights Matrix, the Priority Kill Switch, and the Seam Handoff Standard—to realign your teams, re-establish trust, and build a resilient culture that can weather any storm.
