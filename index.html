Leadership Drift

Recognizing the signs, correcting the course, and re-anchoring your organizational culture.

Why do high-performing organizations suddenly find themselves bogged down by execution delays and internal friction? The answer rarely lies in catastrophic failure, but rather in a slow, almost imperceptible process: Leadership Drift.

Drawing on more than two decades of senior operational leadership, Kristen Peloquin delivers both an uncompromising diagnosis of systemic drift and four tangible operating frameworks to restore velocity, accountability, and cultural resilience.
