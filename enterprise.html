Implementation Framework

The 4-Phase Operational Implementation Roadmap

How we systematically embed Leadership Drift frameworks into your team's everyday execution rhythm.

Phase 01
Diagnostics & Discovery

Operational Friction Audit

Before gathering the leadership team, we conduct an audit of active cross-departmental seams, consensus bottlenecks, and priority creep. This grounds the upcoming sessions in objective operational realities rather than theoretical debates.

Phase 02
Alignment & Agreements

Executive Alignment Offsite

Intensive, facilitated sessions (in-person or virtual) where leaders map out their Decision Architecture, establish the Rule of the Single "A", negotiate behavioral contracts, and execute capacity trade-offs using the Priority Kill Switch.

Phase 03
Codification & Standards

Escalation & Seam Standards

We codify the Seam Handoff Standard between key departments (e.g., Sales to Operations, Product to Engineering) and institute the 48-Hour Feedback Log to decouple performance feedback from personal friction.

Phase 04
Institutionalization

Operating Cadence Handover

The operating frameworks are transitioned into daily operations—embedded into weekly tactical standups, monthly operations reviews, and quarterly governance retrospectives to guarantee drift does not return.
