The Architecture of Organizational Drift

Based on the diagnostic frameworks in Leadership Drift by Kristen Peloquin, operational breakdown occurs across four predictable seams:

System One

Decision Architecture

Eliminating consensus paralysis, live rescues, and bottlenecked calendars through the Rule of the Single ‘A’.

System Two

Accountability Loop

Decoupling performance feedback from interpersonal conflict using uncancelable 1-on-1s and the 48-Hour Feedback Log.

System Three

Capacity Blueprint

Protecting high performers and halting hidden burnout with the strict Priority Kill Switch (1-in, 1-out).

System Four

Rules of Engagement

Resolving interdepartmental friction, turf disputes, and dropped handoffs through standardized Seam Handoff Standards.
