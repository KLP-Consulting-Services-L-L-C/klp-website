Performance Management Systems

Traditional annual performance reviews happen too late to fix drifting execution and only create anxiety. Kristen partners with leadership teams to design and install modern, continuous performance systems that decouple personalities from standards, establish objective feedback rhythms, and build a culture of mutual accountability.
